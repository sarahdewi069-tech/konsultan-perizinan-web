Who We Are

A licensing consultant focused on one thing: a clear, accountable process.

AD SNI Consultant is a consultant engaged in certification and business licensing services for companies in Indonesia. Since starting operations in 2019, we have supported companies across various sectors — from manufacturing, importers, to service providers — in meeting regulatory requirements such as SNI, BPOM, TKDN, NPB, Import License, RKP-RPL, and various other business licenses.

Our clients include domestic companies as well as companies with cross-border affiliations or business activities, with a total of more than 200 clients supported to date — including companies from China and other countries.

Credibility in the eyes of compliance and legal teams is built through process consistency — not claims.

Our Approach

How we work with your internal team.

1

Assessment Based on Actual Needs

We don't offer every license type generically. Every engagement starts by mapping your product, business activity, and the regulations genuinely relevant to your company.

2

Documented Coordination

Every stage of the process — from required documents to submission status — is communicated regularly to your internal team, so it can be verified at any time.

3

Keeping Up With Regulatory Changes

SNI, BPOM, and TKDN requirements can change over time. We ensure the approach used refers to the regulations in effect while the process is running.

4

Direct Communication, Not Layered

Your company communicates directly with the consultant handling your case, not through a long chain of teams.
