1. Data We Collect

When you fill out the contact form or reach us via WhatsApp on this site, we may receive the following data: name, company name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, service of interest, and the details of your needs that you provide.

Data you submit through the contact form is forwarded directly to our WhatsApp and is not stored in a database on this site's server. Further communication follows the privacy policy of the platform used (e.g. WhatsApp/Meta).

You have the right to request that we delete data you have previously submitted through the contact form, by contacting us directly using the contact details below.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us via email at ademasnun70@gmail.com or WhatsApp.
