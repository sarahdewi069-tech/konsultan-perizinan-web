A General Guide to Obtaining BPOM Marketing Authorization for Companies

BPOM

For companies engaged in processed food, traditional medicine, cosmetics, or health supplements, marketing authorization from the National Agency of Drug and Food Control (BPOM) is a legal requirement before a product can be officially sold or distributed in Indonesia. This article discusses a general overview of that process from a company's perspective, not a detailed technical bureaucratic guide.

Product Categories Requiring BPOM Marketing Authorization

  • Processed food (both domestically produced and imported)
  • Traditional medicine and herbal products
  • Cosmetics
  • Health supplements

Each category has a different registration pathway, data requirements, and technical verification institution.

Considerations Before Applying

Before submitting a registration, a company should confirm the following:

  • Product classification is accurate — a classification error can lead to the wrong registration pathway and add processing time.
  • Business legality is complete — including the Business ID Number (NIB) and business permits relevant to the product category.
  • Formulation and raw materials are documented — including the origin of raw materials and their compliance with the provisions applicable to that product category.
  • Label design complies with provisions — including claims stated on the packaging, as non-compliant claims can hold up the registration process.

Why This Process Often Takes Longer Than Expected

Many companies, especially SMEs applying for marketing authorization for the first time, experience delays because supporting documents were incomplete from the start, or because the product formulation needed revision to comply with requirements. Preparing all documents thoroughly before initial submission generally helps speed up the overall process compared to submitting with incomplete documents.

BPOM registration provisions can differ depending on the product category and may be updated over time. This article is general information, not a binding technical guide.

Summary

BPOM marketing authorization is a legal requirement, not merely an administrative formality. Thorough document preparation from the start — from product classification to formulation completeness — is the main factor determining a smooth registration process.
