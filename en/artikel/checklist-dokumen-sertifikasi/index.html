Document Checklist Before Applying for Corporate Certification

General Guide

While each type of certification and business license has different specific requirements, there are a number of baseline documents almost always requested across various licensing processes. Preparing these documents early can speed up the overall process.

Company Legality Documents

  • Business Identification Number (NIB)
  • Company deed of establishment and any amendments (if applicable)
  • Company Taxpayer Identification Number (NPWP)
  • Business or operating permits relevant to the business field

Product or Service-Related Documents

  • Product technical specifications (for certification such as SNI, BPOM, or NPB)
  • Production process data or cost structure (for TKDN calculation)
  • Expert personnel qualification and experience documents, if required by the license type being applied for
  • Business activity data and potential environmental impact (for RKP-RPL)

Other Frequently Requested Supporting Documents

  • Power of attorney, if the process is delegated to a consultant or third party
  • Proof of ownership or lease of the business location
  • Previous product testing result documents, if any have been conducted

Why This Checklist Should Be Prepared Early

Most delays in the certification and licensing process are not caused by slow institutional processes, but by incomplete supporting documents at the time of initial submission, resulting in repeated requests for additional data. Conducting an internal audit of the documents above before starting the submission can significantly cut processing time.

The document list above is general in nature. Specific requirements still need to be adjusted to the type of certification/license being applied for and the provisions in effect at the time of submission.

Summary

Baseline document completeness is the foundation of an efficient licensing process. Preparing legality documents, product data, and supporting documents from the start helps companies avoid a prolonged process.
